Cybersecurity Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from cyber threats. It's important because it prevents data breaches, identity theft, and other types of cybercrime. What does cybersecurity protect? Data: Protects sensitive information from being accessed, changed, or destroyed Systems: Protects computers, networks, and programs from unauthorized access or attacks Services: Ensures that services continue to operate normally How does cybersecurity work? Technology: Uses tools and practices to reduce the impact of cyber threats People: Users must understand and comply with data protection principles Processes: Organizations can use threat management systems to automate security operations How can you protect your data? Use strong passwords, ideally 12 characters long and using letters, numbers, and symbols Use a password manager to keep track of your passwords Enable two-factor authentication Back up your data regularly, and store backups in multiple locations Keep your software up to date, and apply security patches as soon as they're available.
